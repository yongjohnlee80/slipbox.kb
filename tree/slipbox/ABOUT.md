# slipbox/

- **Holds:** slips: one idea each, in full sentences, in your own words.
- **Naming:** `<YYYYMMDDHHmm> <short title>.md`; the timestamp is the slip's `id`.
- **Type:** `slip`, with `id` required. `status` draft or final.
- **Rules:** flat, with no subfolders. A slip links to the slips it continues, supports or contradicts; an index slip (`kind: index`) is an entry point to a topic.
